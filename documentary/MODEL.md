# GardenOS — The Model (six layers · four zones · events)

> The artistic North Star, from Andy (2026-06-06). The garden is not a collection
> of plants — it is a **collection of ecological layers moving through time**. The
> simulation's unit is the **event**, not the plant. This supersedes the flat
> single-season-curve approach of Trials 06–07; those proved the *look* (first-person,
> brush-stroke plants, Nocturne, roundness). This document is what they now serve.

This is a specific real garden — **London N1** — not a generic one. That specificity
is the point: it should feel like *this* garden experienced over a year.

## The six overlapping systems

### 1 · Canopy Layer (the trees) — *drives the whole simulation*
Bay tree, Rhododendron, Ceanothus, Hydrangea (shrub). They **cast shade, drop
leaves, create seasonal light, moisture zones, wind shadows**. The bay tree creates
the woodland floor that lives underneath it.
Visual: **dappled shade moving across the ground**, autumn leaf fall, branch
silhouettes, pollen/blossom particles.

### 2 · Woodland Floor — *the masterpiece zone, artistically*
Epimedium, Pulmonaria, Hellebores, Brunnera, Tellima, Bergenia, Polygonatum
(Solomon's Seal), Rodgersia, Tricyrtis, Ajuga reptans, Heucheras, Ferns.
- **Spring:** Brunnera blue clouds, Pulmonaria flowers, Epimedium floating blooms
- **Summer:** ferns unfurling, big Rodgersia leaves, woodland green mass
- **Autumn:** bay leaves falling, fern collapse
- **Winter:** skeleton stems, evergreen Bergenia, Hellebores
→ "almost a low-poly magical forest floor."

### 3 · Vertical Accent Layer — *the drama / motion*
Foxgloves, Veronicastrum, Verbascum, Ammi majus, Agapanthus (planned).
Rapid spring emergence, flower spikes, wind movement. "Hundreds of glowing foxglove
towers emerging in May." (The Trial 07 spires/umbels already gesture at this.)

### 4 · Pollinator Layer — *London gardens hum*
Bumblebees, honey bees, hoverflies, butterflies. Cluster around Nepeta, Foxgloves,
Ammi, Lobelia, Agapanthus. **Density changes through the year.**

### 5 · Seasonal Bulb Layer — *annual pulses*
Crocus, Snowdrops, Alliums, Daffodils.
Jan empty → Feb shoots → Mar crocus → May allium spheres floating → Jun collapse.

### 6 · Human Layer — *where it becomes art*
Never show people. Show traces: watering can passes, hose events, morning coffee,
cat crossing, washing in the wind, patio lights at dusk. Tiny signs of life.

## The four zones (London N1)

| Zone | Character | Planting |
|------|-----------|----------|
| **A · Front Garden** | formal, street-facing | Ceanothus, Hydrangea, Rhododendron, Lobelia drifts, Alliums |
| **B · Woodland Garden** | the masterpiece, soft shade | Ferns, Brunnera, Foxgloves, Solomon's Seal, Rodgersia, Ajuga, Heucheras |
| **C · Patio** | most colourful, pots | Lobelia, Bacopa, Ipomoea, seasonal bedding |
| **D · Lawn** | *not a lawn — an ecosystem* | moss, clover, grass, self-seeders; changes with drought/rain |

## Simulate events, not plants

The clock is a list of events the year moves through:

> first bee sighting · foxglove emergence · bay leaf drop · summer drought ·
> heavy rain · hydrangea bloom · autumn seed fall · frost · fern unfurling · evening shade

**The artwork is a time machine.** Press ↑ → one year passes in ~30 seconds. The
garden grows, flowers, collapses, reseeds, sleeps, begins again. Closer to how you
*experience* the garden than any literal 3D replica.

## How the trials map onto this

- 06–07d built **Layers 2 & 3** (woodland floor mass + vertical accents) as *look*,
  Nocturne, brush-stroke plants, roundness — but on a flat single season curve.
- **Trial 08 →** introduce **Layer 1 (Canopy: dappled moving shade + leaf-fall)** —
  the layer that drives the rest — plus **Layer 4 (Pollinators)** as the first
  *event-driven, time-moving* systems. This is the turn from "a garden" to "layers
  through time."
- Later: zones A–D as spatial regions with their own planting; bulb pulses; the
  human-trace layer; and finally the Trial 05 substrate driving inter-layer dynamics
  (shade → floor composition) so succession is emergent, not scripted.
